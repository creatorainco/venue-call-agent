1. **The endpointing tail is gone.** Short greetings ("Hello?", "Yes?", "Golden Crane.") now end
   on the interim 600 ms after it stands still instead of waiting 6 to 10 s for the recogniser's
   final in the noise bed; keyword-cued turns end at 450 ms. The room's babble writes interims too
   ("happy birthday"), and the first run of this change answered them; the foreground gate (9 dB
   over the room's floor) logged `turn_end room_only` for every one of those in this run, and no
   turn was answered from the bed.
2. **SUPERSHORT does nothing on `telephony`.** Measured on all 47 clips with interim results on:
   SHORT p50 1117 / p90 2177 ms, SUPERSHORT p50 1173 / p90 2170 ms. Kept SHORT; the short hello is
   ended by us.
3. **Dead air p90 is a tie, and it is not reply latency.** The five calls at 7.5 s and over are all
   `closed by client`: the venue's script ran out and the fake venue waited its `endWaitMs` for a
   hangup while the agent waited for the venue (unknown-question, wants-to-cancel,
   talks-over-most-of-disclosure, booking-hello, money-covered-amount, the same calls as the
   baseline). The 14020 ms is money-minimum-spend: the recogniser never finalised "Alright,
   noted." and a goodbye is never ended on the interim (it ends the call, and "thanks" is the head
   of "thanks, but who's paying?"). Closing a call after the venue goes quiet is agent behaviour,
   not PERF-A; it is the next lever for this number.
4. **Cold start: the code cannot reach 1000 ms; min-instances can.** Dev probe 1 found the service
   scaled to zero: 3633 ms from connect to upgrade (2258 ms in the baseline: container start
   varies). The local cold path is the disclosure's own synthesis, about 1.0 s for that sentence
   on Chirp 3 HD whether or not TLS is already open (measured: first synthesis 1031-1080 ms,
   preconnect 68-84 ms of it), and it cannot be rendered before the backend has sent the sentence
   on the first call a process takes. The warm-up now retries inside the request, so a fresh
   Cloud Run instance no longer leaves its fixed phrases cold (`cascade_warm_failed
   TtsUnavailable` was the boot log). Closing the gap needs an infra flag the owner decides:
   `--min-instances 1` removes both halves (DEPLOY.md §3 cost); `--no-cpu-throttling` alone does
   not remove the container start.
5. **Intent fallbacks (17 of 49) are the next stage.** The 900 ms timeout still sits on the
   model's p90 (probe p90 765 ms, p99 3886 ms). The prefetch starts the model 250 ms after an
   interim settles, which is why intent p50 fell to 461 ms; the timeout itself is unchanged.
