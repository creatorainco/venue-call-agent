## Findings (written by hand after the run)

**The gate fails on all three readings. Per the plan this report merges as the measurement.**

1. **The slowest stage is endpointing: waiting for the recogniser to call the venue's turn over.**
   Read off the wire (first audio less the runtime's own time after the STT final), it is p50
   1356 ms, p90 6219 ms, max 10319 ms over 39 paired replies, against a runtime that needs p50
   906 ms, p90 1027 ms from the final to its first frame out. The long tail is short turns in a
   loud room: a one- or two-word greeting ("Hello?", "Yes?", "Golden Crane.") with babble behind
   it took 6.2 to 10.3 s to finalise (`stop-calling`, `wrong-number`, `money-covered-amount`,
   `phone-menu`). Google's SHORT endpointing waits for silence, and a restaurant never goes
   silent. That is where the p90 goes. Next lever (I3P-13 and the TASK-3749 matrix): finalise on
   our own end-of-turn (the VAD) instead of waiting for STT's, and compare `chirp_3` and
   SUPERSHORT, on this corpus.
2. **Intent is the second stage: p50 844 ms, p90 907 ms, and 13 of 26 model turns fell back.**
   In the call, the pinned `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` answered inside the 900 ms timeout only half the
   time; out of the call (same lines, 10 s ceiling) it was p50 681 ms, p90 929 ms, p99 4284 ms, 32
   of 36 inside 900 ms. So the timeout sits right on the model's p90, and every fallback costs the
   whole 900 ms and turns into an "unclear" reply. Keyword cues decided 20 turns in about 0 ms.
3. **Cold start is 3613 ms against a 1000 ms limit; warm it is 3 ms.** Cloud Run's own log
   confirms probe 1 hit a fresh instance: "Starting new instance" 01:25:51.38Z, `listening`
   01:25:52.69Z (1.31 s), `cascade_warm` 01:25:54.11Z. The client saw 2258 ms to the WebSocket
   upgrade. A process that takes a call before its warm-up finishes then needs 1355 ms from `start`
   to the disclosure (the disclosure's synthesis is not cached yet); once warm it is 2 to 4 ms. A
   minimum of one warm instance removes both halves; that is a monthly cost and the owner's call
   (docs/DEPLOY.md §3).
4. **Disclosure integrity held on every call.** 20 of 20 calls played the full disclosure (6140 ms)
   before anything else; transcribed, each matched the scenario's sentence at word recall 0.93
   (the miss is the brand name split into two words). No audio after the venue hung up on any call.
5. **The runtime's own `venue_end_ms` is not usable yet** (`endpointing` p50 39 ms): under the noise
   bed the VAD never reports the end of the venue's turn, so the runtime stamps the end at the
   final. The wire figure above is the one to read until the VAD is tuned for a noisy room.
